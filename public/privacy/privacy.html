Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Dual Translate ("we," "us," or "our") is a real-time dual-language translation application powered by Google's Gemini AI models. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect information provided through Firebase authentication, including:

  • Email address
  • Display name (if provided)
  • Authentication provider details (email/password or Google account)

1.2 Usage Data

We collect data necessary for the application to function, including:

  • Translation history and conversation logs stored in Firebase Realtime Database
  • Language pair selections and settings preferences
  • Application performance metrics and error reports
  • Device information and browser type for compatibility purposes

1.3 Audio Data

The application requires microphone access for voice-based translations. Audio data is processed in real-time through Google's Live API and is not permanently stored on our servers after processing.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and improve real-time translation services
  • Authenticate and manage user accounts
  • Store your translation history for your personal reference
  • Synchronize your settings across devices
  • Maintain application security and prevent abuse
  • Communicate important service updates

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase, including:

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages user identity securely
  • Firebase Realtime Database: Stores translation history and user preferences

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256). Access to the database is restricted through Firebase Security Rules tied to user authentication state.

4. Third-Party Services

Dual Translate relies on the following third-party services:

  • Google Gemini AI: Translation processing uses Google's Gemini language models. Text sent for translation may be processed by Google's servers according to their Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase: Provides authentication and real-time database services under Google's Privacy Policy.

We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for additional details on their data handling practices.

5. Data Retention

We retain your translation history and account data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and associated data at any time through the application settings or by contacting us directly.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access, rectify, or delete your personal data
  • Request export of your data in a portable format
  • Restrict or object to certain processing activities
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Request restriction or deletion of your data

To exercise these rights, please contact us at support@dualtranslate.app.

7. Children's Privacy

Dual Translate is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware of such collection, we will take steps to delete the information.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside your country of residence. Google Firebase and Gemini AI operate globally, and by using this application, you consent to the transfer of your data to these jurisdictions.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes via email or through the application. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:
